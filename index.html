This model uses Black-Scholes pricing to estimate what your option would theoretically be worth on the sell date you choose, based on the stock price, implied volatility, and time remaining. Delta, gamma, theta, and vega shown above are calculated from these inputs, not entered — they reflect the sensitivities at your chosen scenario and will shift as price, time, and volatility change. Real market prices can differ due to bid/ask spreads, liquidity, volatility skew, and other factors — treat this as an estimate for planning, not a quote.
